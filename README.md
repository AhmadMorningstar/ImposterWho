# 🕵️‍♂️ Imposter Who? (کێ دژمنە؟)

[![Platform](https://img.shields.io/badge/Platform-Android-green?logo=android)](https://play.google.com/store)
[![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-All_Rights_Reserved-red)](#)

A fast-paced, local multiplayer social deduction game designed for friends and family! Discover who the imposter is before it's too late. 🚀

## 📥 Download Early Access (Open Testing)

To play **Imposter Who**, follow these three steps using the **same Google Account**:

1. **Join the Community** [Join the Google Group](https://groups.google.com/g/imposterwho) (Required for access).
   
2. **Opt-in for Testing** [Sign up for Early Access](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.ahmadmorningstar.imposterwho) on the Google Play Store.

3. **Install the Game** [Download on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ahmadmorningstar.imposterwho) once the steps above are complete.

> **Note:** If the Play Store link shows "Item not found," ensure you have joined the Google Group in Step 1 first!

---

## ✨ Features
- 🌍 **Trilingual Support:** Play in **Kurdish**, **English**, or **Arabic**!
- 🎨 **Dynamic Themes:** Choose from Midnight, Crimson, Emerald, or Gold.
- 🎭 **100+ Words:** Categories ranging from Food & Drinks to Nature and Places.
- 🔒 **Privacy Focused:** No data leaves your device. Play offline, anywhere!

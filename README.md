## ✨ Features
- 🌍 **Trilingual Support:** Play in **Kurdish**, **English**, or **Arabic**!
- 🎨 **Dynamic Themes:** Choose from Midnight, Crimson, Emerald, or Gold.
- 🎭 **100+ Words:** Categories ranging from Food & Drinks to Nature and Places.
- 🔒 **Privacy Focused:** No data leaves your device. Play offline, anywhere!
